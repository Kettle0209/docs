Every Wield payout reaches you in INR with documentation in your name. This page explains that document and what to take to your CA.

<Warning>
  Wield does not provide tax advice, and nothing here is tax advice. How your income is classified and taxed depends on your circumstances and is a question for a qualified chartered accountant. We provide the documentation; your CA determines the treatment.
</Warning>

## The document you receive

Every payout comes with an **eFIRA** — an electronic Foreign Inward Remittance Advice — issued in your name through Xflow, Wield's RBI-authorised payment partner.

An eFIRA is an official bank record of an inward remittance. It records the INR amount, the date, the sender (a company outside India), and you as the recipient, with the remittance described as payment for a service. It is the same class of record that exporters, freelancers, and consultants receive when a client abroad pays them.

## What to give your CA

Bring these to your CA, who can advise on how the income should be classified and reported in your return:

- Your **eFIRA** for each payout
- Your **payout history** from Wield
- Your **Contributor Agreement**, which sets out what Wield pays you for

Your CA may also want details of how you paid your evaluation fees. Keep your payment records.
