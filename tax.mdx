Your payout reaches you in INR with documentation in your name. This page explains that document and what to give your CA.

<Warning>
  This is not tax advice. Consult your CA.
</Warning>

## The document you receive

Every payout comes with an **eFIRA** — an electronic Foreign Inward Remittance Advice — issued in your name through Xflow, Wield's RBI-authorised payment partner.

An eFIRA is an official bank record. It shows your INR as a payment for a service, sent from a company outside India, and names the amount, the date, and you as the person paid. It is the same record IT exporters and freelancers receive when a client abroad pays them for their work.

## What it means for tax

Because it is documented as a service payment from abroad, your income is **professional-services income**, not a crypto gain. That is a different category from the flat 30% on crypto gains — and for many people, a lower one. Where you land depends on your total income, which is why it is your CA's call.

## What to give your CA

- Your **eFIRA** for each payout
- Your **payout history** from Wield
