Pass a skill-based evaluation, earn a trading account without depositing your own capital, and withdraw earnings to your bank in INR — at any time.

## The process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Take an evaluation">
    Choose your account size (\$10,000–\$200,000) and start your evaluation. Trade to reach the profit target while staying within the risk parameters — in your own style.

    <Check>
      Reach the target in a single trade. Consistency rules do not apply.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Trading Account">
    Pass your evaluation and your trading account activates immediately — no manual review. There is no profit target from here. Keep trading in your own style, within the same drawdown limits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get paid in INR">
    Withdraw your payouts at any time. Your profit is converted to INR and sent to your bank through Xflow, our RBI-authorised partner.

    <Info>
      **Up to 80% profit share.** Payouts are processed within 24 hours. Your tax documentation (eFIRA) is emailed to you within 24 hours of the bank deposit.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Trading on Wield is simulated and runs on live market data — no real assets are bought or sold, and none of your own capital is in the account. The profit you withdraw in INR is real.
</Note>

## Risk parameters

Every account size trades to the same profit target, within the same risk parameters.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Maximum loss | 10% of account size (fixed floor) |
| Maximum daily loss | 5% of account size, per IST day |

<Info>
  **Profit target: 8%.** **Maximum leverage: 15×.**
</Info>

## In your trading account

Once you pass, you trade under the same parameters as the evaluation. The key differences:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="No profit target">
    There is no cap on how much you can earn. Keep trading as long as you stay within the risk limits.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Payouts on demand">
    Request payouts at any time, including more than once a day. Up to 80% profit share, settled in INR to your bank through Xflow.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
