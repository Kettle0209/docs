Pass a skill-based evaluation, earn a funded-size trading account, and withdraw your payout to your bank in INR — at any time.

## The process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Take an evaluation">
    Choose your account size (\$10,000–\$200,000) and start your evaluation. Trade to reach the profit target while staying within the risk parameters — in your own style. Evaluations start from a one-time fee — see [Evaluations](/evaluations).

    <Check>
      A single trade can satisfy the target — no minimum number of trading days, and no consistency rules.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access your trading account">
    Pass your evaluation and your trading account activates immediately — no manual review. There is no profit target from here. Keep trading in your own style, within the same drawdown limits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get paid in INR">
    Withdraw your payout at any time. It is converted to INR and sent to your bank through Xflow, our RBI-authorised partner.

    <Info>
      **Up to 80% payout.** Payouts are processed within 24 hours. Your remittance documentation (eFIRA) is emailed to you within 24 hours of the bank deposit.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Trading on Wield is simulated and runs on live market data — no real assets are bought or sold, and none of your own capital is in the account. The payout you withdraw in INR is real.
</Note>

## How your payout works

Your payout is a performance-based Service Fee: Wield pays you for your market analysis, scaled to its assessed value, at up to 80%. In prop terms, that is an up-to-80% payout on your account's performance — settled to your bank in INR through Xflow. Full terms are in the [Contributor Agreement](https://wieldhq.com/legal/contributor-agreement).

## Risk parameters

Every account size trades to the same profit target, within the same risk parameters.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Maximum loss | 10% of account size (fixed floor) |
| Maximum daily loss | 5% of account size, per IST day |

<Info>
  **Profit target: 8%.** **Maximum leverage: 5× (2× on high-volatility markets).**
</Info>
