## The basics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the trading simulated or real markets?">
    You trade in a simulated environment running on live market data — no real assets are bought or sold, and no order reaches an exchange. The money you withdraw is real: your payout is paid to your bank in INR. See [Platform Overview](/platform).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I risk my own money?">
    Beyond the one-time evaluation fee, none of your own money is ever in an account. See [How It Works](/how-it-works).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the platform live yet?">
    Not yet. Wield is in early access, targeted to launch between 10 and 15 July 2026. You can reserve your access now, with a fee that's refundable any time before launch. See [Early Access](/early-access).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting paid

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get paid?">
    You withdraw your payout in INR, to your Indian bank account, through Xflow — an RBI-authorised payment partner — with an eFIRA issued in your name. See [Payouts](/platform/payouts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How fast are payouts?">
    Processing is typically under 24 hours; bank settlement after that follows normal Indian banking timelines. After your first payout, there's no window, no minimum, and no limit on how often you withdraw.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will my bank account get frozen?">
    No payment method can promise a bank will never query a transfer — and anyone who tells you otherwise isn't being straight with you. What Wield gives you is documentation: every payout has an identifiable sender, comes through a regulated rail, and arrives with an eFIRA in your name — an on-record remittance, not an undocumented transfer from an anonymous counterparty. See [Payouts](/platform/payouts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is the money taxed?">
    Each payout comes with an eFIRA recording it as an inward remittance for a service, from a company outside India. How that income is classified and taxed depends on your circumstances, and your CA determines the treatment. This isn't tax advice. See [Tax & eFIRA](/tax).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Evaluations and accounts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How hard is it to pass?">
    The evaluation is a real test of skill, and many people don't pass. You reach an 8% profit target without breaching a 10% maximum loss or a 5% daily loss. See [Evaluations](/evaluations).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I don't pass?">
    The evaluation ends and you owe nothing further. There's no penalty beyond the evaluation ending, and you can take another whenever you want.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I pass?">
    Passing is recognised automatically when you reach the target within the limits — no manual review. You earn a trading account at the size you chose, with no profit target and the same risk limits. See [Trading Accounts](/trading-accounts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What ends a trading account?">
    Reaching your maximum loss, reaching your maximum daily loss, breaking the Acceptable Use Policy, or choosing to stop. If your account ends on a breach, there's a reduced-fee return path — see [Trading Accounts](/trading-accounts) for the current terms.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I hold more than one account?">
    Yes. You can choose any size, take more than one evaluation, and hold several accounts at once, in different sizes. See [Progression](/progression).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
