## The basics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the trading simulated or real markets?">
    You trade in a simulated environment that runs on live market data — no real assets are bought or sold, and no order reaches an exchange. The money you withdraw is real: your profit share is paid to your bank in INR. See [The Wield Platform](/platform).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I risk my own money?">
    No. You pay a one-time fee for an evaluation. Beyond that fee, none of your own money is ever in an account. See [How it works](/how-it-works).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the platform live yet?">
    Not yet. Wield is in early access and the platform launches by 15 July 2026. You can secure your access now, with a fee that is refundable any time before launch. See [Early Access](/early-access).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting paid

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get paid?">
    You withdraw your profit share on demand, in INR, to your Indian bank account. It is sent through Xflow, an RBI-authorised payment partner, with an eFIRA issued in your name. See [Payouts](/payouts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How fast are payouts?">
    Wield's processing time is typically under 24 hours. Bank settlement after that follows normal Indian banking timelines. There is no payout window, no minimum, and no limit on how often you withdraw.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will my bank account get frozen?">
    No payment method can promise a bank will never query a transfer. What Wield gives you is documented provenance: each payout has an identifiable sender, a regulated rail, and an eFIRA in your name — unlike INR received from an anonymous P2P counterparty. See [Payouts](/payouts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is the money taxed?">
    Your payout is documented as a service payment from a company outside India, which places it as professional-services income rather than a crypto gain. This is not tax advice — your CA confirms what applies to you. See [Tax & eFIRA](/tax).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Evaluations and accounts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How hard is it to pass?">
    The evaluation is a real test of skill. Many people do not pass. You reach an 8% profit target without breaching a 10% maximum loss or a 5% daily loss. See [Evaluations](/evaluations).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I don't pass?">
    The evaluation ends and you owe nothing further. There is no penalty beyond the evaluation ending, and you can take another whenever you want.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I pass?">
    Passing is recognised automatically when you reach the profit target within the limits — no manual review. You earn a trading account at the size you chose, with no profit target and the same risk limits. See [Trading Accounts](/trading-accounts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What ends a trading account?">
    Reaching your maximum loss, reaching your maximum daily loss, breaking the Acceptable Use Policy, or choosing to stop. If your account ends on a breach, you can start again at the same size within 30 days at half the fee. See [Trading Accounts](/trading-accounts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I hold more than one account?">
    Yes. You can choose any account size, take more than one evaluation, and hold several accounts at the same time, in different sizes. See [Scaling](/scaling).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
