You can reach a larger account two ways on Wield: take an evaluation at the size you want, or earn a size-up over time as a performance perk. Neither requires starting small and grinding upward — every size is open from day one.

## Earning a larger account

If you trade a steady, within-limits record over a meaningful period, Wield may offer you an account at the next size up — at no cost, with the same rules.

The sizes run in this ladder:

\$10,000 → \$25,000 → \$50,000 → \$100,000 → \$200,000

<Note>
  A size-up is a perk, not a payout decision. Your payouts are never discretionary — see [Payouts](/platform/payouts). The free progression offer is a bonus we extend based on a trader's record; it is not automatic, not on a fixed schedule, and offered at our discretion. When it is offered and you accept, the new size applies from then on.
</Note>

You can also reach any size immediately by taking an evaluation at that size — you never have to wait for a progression offer to trade larger.

## Choosing your size, and running more than one

Every account size is available from the start, so a larger account is simply a matter of taking an evaluation at that size. You can also hold several accounts at once, in different sizes — for example, to run different strategies in parallel. See [Evaluations](/evaluations) for sizes and fees.
