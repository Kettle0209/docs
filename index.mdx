Wield is trading infrastructure for skill-based capital, built for Indian traders.

In plain terms: you prove your trading in an evaluation, earn a trading account you do not fund yourself, and keep a share of the profit you make — paid to your bank in INR, with proper documentation, on demand.

## Who it is for

Wield is built for traders who already know what they are doing and want to trade larger than their own capital allows — without depositing their own money, and without the payout problems that come with moving crypto in and out of an Indian bank account.

You bring the skill. Wield provides the account, the platform, and a way to get paid in documented INR.

## What to know upfront

A few things stated plainly, so nothing comes as a surprise:

- **You trade in a simulated environment** that runs on live market data. No real assets are bought or sold, and no order reaches an exchange. None of your own money is ever in the account. Your profit share, paid in INR, is real.
- **A trading account is earned, not bought.** The fee is for the evaluation. Passing is a real test of skill, and many people do not pass.
- **You are paid for the analysis you provide**, as professional-services income, documented in your name. How it is taxed depends on your situation and is for your CA to confirm. See [Tax & eFIRA](/tax).

Wield is operated by Vipas Ventures MB, a registered company you can verify before you pay anything. Details are on the [Payouts](/payouts) page and in the footer.
