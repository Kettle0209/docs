Wield is infrastructure for skill-based capital, built for Indian crypto traders. Profits are withdrawn on demand and paid to your bank as documented INR — professional-services income from an identifiable EU entity, not anonymous P2P. Your CA can confirm the treatment.

The terminal runs on live market data with TradingView charts — a fast, responsive trading environment, with none of your own capital at risk.
