## Who is this for?

Wield is for experienced crypto traders in India — people who already trade perpetuals and understand leverage, drawdown, and risk. The goal is simple: trade a larger account without putting up the capital yourself, and be paid in INR you can account for.

If you have moved profits through P2P or offshore accounts, you know the freeze risk that comes with them. Here, your earnings arrive as documented INR — in your own bank, in your own name.
