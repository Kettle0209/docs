Wield is crypto-native — the platform trades crypto perpetual futures, and nothing else. Markets are quoted in USDT and trade 24/7, the same as the underlying crypto market.

Pairs are organised into three tiers. Tier 1 and Tier 2 are a fixed core set, always available. Tier 3 is a dynamic high-volatility set that refreshes by trading volume. Leverage steps down as volatility rises.

## Tier 3 — High volatility

Up to **2×** leverage.

Tier 3 is defined dynamically: the top 50 USDT-margined perpetual futures by 24-hour volume across major exchanges, excluding everything already in Tier 1 and Tier 2, refreshed periodically by volume ranking. Because it's volume-driven, the exact list changes over time — the live set is always shown in the terminal.
