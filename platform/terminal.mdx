The trading terminal runs on live market data, with TradingView charts and prices sourced from major exchanges. It is built to be fast and responsive.

<Note>
  Trading on Wield is simulated and runs on live market data — no real assets are bought or sold, and none of your own capital is in the account. The payout you withdraw in INR is real.
</Note>
