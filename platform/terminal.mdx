The Wield terminal is a crypto-native trading environment built around live market data. You get TradingView Advanced Charts, the order and risk tools you'd expect from a serious platform, and full control over how you open, manage, and close positions.

<Note>
  Execution on Wield is simulated and runs on live market data — your orders are filled against a live price feed, but no real assets are bought or sold and none of your own capital is in the account. Your results drive the assessment of your analysis; the payout you withdraw in INR is real.
</Note>

## Market data

Live crypto prices are sourced from major exchanges and update in real time, so you trade against the market as it actually moves. The terminal's charts, prices, and your open-position results all update from the same live feed.

## Leverage

Leverage runs up to **5×**, stepping down to **2×** on high-volatility markets. The limit for each pair is set by its tier — see [Markets](/platform/markets).

## Markets

Wield is crypto-native — crypto perpetual futures only, not a multi-asset platform spread thin across equities and commodities. The full pair list and the leverage limit for each tier are on [Markets](/platform/markets).

## PnL Share Cards

Generate a PnL Share Card of any closed trade or evaluation result, with your stats and payout details. Each card carries a verifiable QR code anyone can scan to confirm it's real — proof, not just a screenshot.
