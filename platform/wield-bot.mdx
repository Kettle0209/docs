Everything starts in the Wield bot on Telegram. Opening it commits you to nothing — it's a private chat where you can check who Wield is, get straight answers, and start an evaluation only when you decide to.

## What you can do in the bot

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Verify who you're dealing with" icon="shield-check">
    The bot gives you Wield's registered operator name and a link to verify it, before you do anything else.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Get your questions answered" icon="circle-question">
    Ask about payouts, how trading works, tax and documentation, or how to join — and go straight to what you care about.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Start an evaluation" icon="play">
    When you're ready, choose an account size and pay the one-time evaluation fee in USDT through NOWPayments.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Stay in the loop" icon="bullhorn">
    The WieldAccess channel is your line to the team and to product updates.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Your access is recorded against your account, so you can pick up where you left off — open the bot, leave, verify on your own time, and come back.
