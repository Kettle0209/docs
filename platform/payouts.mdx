When you are ready to withdraw, you request a payout from your dashboard. Choose an amount, confirm, and your payout is on its way — converted to INR and sent to your bank.

For how the funds reach your bank, the RBI-authorised rail, and the documentation you receive, see [Payouts](/payouts).
