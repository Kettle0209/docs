Wield pays you in INR, directly to your bank. There is no crypto to off-ramp, no wallet to manage, and no P2P step — your payout settles through a regulated remittance rail and arrives as an on-record bank transfer, with a remittance document (eFIRA) for every payment.

<Note>
  Wield runs on simulated execution against a live market feed — no real assets are bought or sold, and none of your own capital is in the account. The payout you withdraw in INR is real.
</Note>

## How a payout works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request your payout">
    From the dashboard, choose an amount available for payout and confirm. There is no minimum withdrawal amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We process it through Xflow">
    The payment is made from Wield — operated by Vipas Ventures MB — to you, an India-resident provider of analysis, through Xflow, our RBI-authorised partner. The amount you withdraw is converted to INR at the prevailing rate, shown to you at the time of each payout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="INR arrives in your bank">
    The funds settle to your bank account in INR, against your PAN, within 24 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You receive your eFIRA">
    An eFIRA is issued in your name for each payment and emailed within 24 hours of the deposit — a record of the payment for your files.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When you can withdraw

Your first payout is available after 5 active days, which gives a sufficient basis for assessing your analysis. After your first payout, you can request payouts on demand — including more than once a day — subject to a balance available for payout, your account remaining in good standing, and normal processing time.

Before your first withdrawal, you complete a one-time identity check (KYC). This protects your account.

## Payouts and your drawdown

Taking a payout does not reduce your loss budget or move your maximum-loss floor. Your risk parameters stay where they are — withdrawing earnings and trading your account are kept separate.

## What an eFIRA is

<Info>
  An eFIRA is an official bank record. It shows that your INR came from a known company outside India, as payment for a service — the same proof IT exporters and freelancers get when they invoice clients abroad.

  Each one names who sent the money, how much, the date, and you as the person who received it.

  It does not stop a bank from asking about a transfer — nothing does. But every payment you receive has a clear sender, a regulated payment route, and a record behind it.
</Info>

## When a payout can be withheld

Earned payouts are paid regardless of how your engagement ends, and they are not subject to discretionary review. The one exception is fraud or misrepresentation: in that case, payment may be held pending investigation. The full terms are set out in the [Contributor Agreement](https://wieldhq.com/legal/contributor-agreement).

## How payouts are taxed

Your payout comes with an eFIRA in your name — an official bank record showing it as a service payment from a company outside India.

How that income is classified and taxed depends on your circumstances and is a question for your CA. Wield provides the documentation; your CA determines the treatment.
