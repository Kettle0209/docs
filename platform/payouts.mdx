Wield pays you in INR, directly to your bank. There is no crypto to off-ramp, no wallet to manage, and no P2P step — your payout settles through a regulated remittance rail and arrives as an on-record bank transfer, with a remittance document (eFIRA) for every payment.

## How a payout works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request your payout">
    From the dashboard, choose an amount available for payout and confirm. There is no minimum withdrawal amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We process it through Xflow">
    Your payout is sent through Xflow, our RBI-authorised payment partner, and converted to INR at the prevailing rate available to Xflow when the payment is initiated. The exact conversion rate is shown to you at the time of each payout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="INR arrives in your bank">
    The funds settle to your bank account in INR, within 24 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You receive your eFIRA">
    Your remittance documentation (eFIRA) is emailed to you within 24 hours of the bank deposit — a record of the payment for your files.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When you can withdraw

Your first payout is available after a minimum activity period, which gives a sufficient basis for assessing your analysis. After your first payout, you can request payouts on demand — including more than once a day — subject to a balance available for payout, your account remaining in good standing, and normal processing time.
