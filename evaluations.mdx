An evaluation is how you earn a Wield trading account. You choose an account size, pay a one-time fee, and trade to reach a profit target while staying within the risk limits. Pass it, and you earn a trading account at that size — with no money of your own deposited.

## The one requirement

To pass, you reach the **profit target** without breaking either risk limit.

You can reach the target in a single trade. There is no minimum number of days to pass, no rule about how much of your profit comes from one day, and no restriction on trading around news or on weekends. How you reach the target is your own.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Profit target | 8% of account size |
| Maximum loss | 10% of account size (fixed floor) |
| Maximum daily loss | 5% of account size, per IST day |
| Time limit | None |
| Minimum days | 5 active days before your first payout, once funded |

The risk limits work the same way as on a trading account. See [Trading Accounts](/trading-accounts) for how the fixed floor and daily limit are measured. As you trade, your live distance to both limits is shown on your dashboard — see [The Wield Platform](/platform).

## Who can take one

Anyone can take an evaluation. There is no trading résumé to submit, no minimum experience, and no approval step before you start. A trading account, though, is earned by passing — it is not bought and not handed out. The evaluation is where you show the trading deserves one.

## When your account is activated

Passing is recognised automatically the moment you reach the profit target within the risk limits. There is no manual review and no waiting for someone to approve you; your trading account is activated for you to continue on.

## Account sizes and fees

The fee is paid once, in USDT, at the INR price shown at checkout. Account size is the reference figure your trading is measured against — not money you deposit.

| Account size | Evaluation fee | Per \$1,000 | Profit share once funded |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| \$10,000 | ₹6,600 | ₹660 | 80% |
| \$25,000 | ₹13,900 | ₹556 | 80% |
| \$50,000 | ₹27,500 | ₹550 | 80% |
| \$100,000 | ₹46,700 | ₹467 | 78% |
| \$200,000 | ₹75,500 | ₹378 | 78% |

The profit share is what you keep once you have a trading account; it is paid to your bank in INR. See [Payouts](/payouts).

## Running more than one account

You can trade more than one account at the same time, in different sizes — useful if you want to run different strategies or sizes in parallel. The per-account fee is lower when you take more than one: the fee is reduced by 10% on two evaluations, and by 15% on three or more.

## Getting back after a breach

If a trading account ends on a breach, you can start again at the same size within 30 days at half the evaluation fee. The point is practical: one bad day should not mean paying full price to return to a level your trading has already reached. It is a return path, not a subscription — each re-entry is a single evaluation like any other.

## Passing is not guaranteed

The evaluation is a real test of skill. Many people do not pass, and reaching an 8% target without breaching a 10% floor or a 5% daily limit takes discipline as well as skill. Each evaluation is judged on its own; how you did on a previous one does not decide the next.
