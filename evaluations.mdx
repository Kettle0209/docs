An evaluation is how you earn a Wield trading account. You choose an account size, pay a one-time fee, and trade to reach a profit target while staying within the risk limits. Pass it, and you earn a trading account at that size — with none of your own capital deposited.

## The one requirement

To pass, you reach the **profit target** without breaking either risk limit.

You can reach the target in a single trade. There is no minimum number of days to pass, no consistency rule about how much of your gain comes from a single day, and no restriction on trading around news or on weekends. How you reach the target is your own.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Profit target | 8% of account size |
| Maximum loss | 10% of account size (fixed floor) |
| Maximum daily loss | 5% of account size, per IST day |
| Maximum leverage | Up to 5× (2× on high-volatility markets) |
| Time limit | None |

The risk limits work the same way as on a trading account. See [Trading Accounts](/trading-accounts) for how the fixed floor and daily limit are measured, and [Markets](/platform/markets) for the leverage limit on each tier. As you trade, your live distance to both limits is shown on your [dashboard](/platform/dashboard).

## Who can take an evaluation

Anyone can. There is no trading résumé to submit, no minimum experience, and no approval step before you start.

## What happens when I pass

Passing is recognised automatically the moment you reach the profit target within the risk limits. There is no manual review and no waiting for someone to approve you — your trading account is activated for you to continue trading on.

## Account sizes and fees

The fee is paid once, in USDT, at the INR price shown at checkout. Account size is the reference figure your trading is measured against — not money you deposit, and the environment is simulated on live market data (see the [Overview](/platform)).

| Account size | Evaluation fee | Per \$1,000 | Payout share once funded |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| \$10,000 | ₹6,600 | ₹660 | 80% |
| \$25,000 | ₹13,900 | ₹556 | 80% |
| \$50,000 | ₹27,500 | ₹550 | 80% |
| \$100,000 | ₹46,700 | ₹467 | 78% |
| \$200,000 | ₹75,500 | ₹378 | 78% |

Once you hold a trading account, you keep the payout share shown above — a performance-based Service Fee, settled to your bank in INR. See [Payouts](/platform/payouts).

## Your first payout

Your first payout is available after a minimum activity period of 5 active days. After that, you can request payouts on demand. This applies once you hold a trading account, not during the evaluation.

## Running more than one account

You can trade more than one account at the same time, in different sizes — useful for running different strategies or sizes in parallel. The per-account fee is reduced by 10% on two evaluations, and by 15% on three or more.

If a trading account later ends on a breach, there is a reduced-fee path to start again at the same size — see [Trading Accounts](/trading-accounts).

## What if I don't pass

If you break a risk limit during the evaluation, that evaluation ends. You owe nothing further, and there is no penalty beyond the evaluation ending. You can take another evaluation whenever you want — each one is judged on its own.

<Warning>
  **Passing is not guaranteed.** The evaluation is a real test of skill. Many people do not pass — reaching an 8% target without breaching a 10% floor or a 5% daily limit takes discipline as well as skill. Each evaluation is judged on its own; how you did on a previous one does not decide the next.
</Warning>
