An evaluation is how you earn a Wield trading account. You choose an account size, pay a one-time fee, and trade to reach a profit target while staying within the risk limits. Pass it, and you earn a trading account at that size — with no money of your own deposited.

## The one requirement

To pass, you reach the **profit target** without breaking either risk limit.

You can reach the target in a single trade. There is no minimum number of days to pass, no rule about how much of your profit comes from one day, and no restriction on trading around news or on weekends. How you reach the target is your own.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Profit target | 8% of account size |
| Maximum loss | 10% of account size (fixed floor) |
| Maximum daily loss | 5% of account size, per IST day |
| Maximum leverage | Up to 5× (2× on high-volatility markets) |
| Time limit | None |
| Minimum days | 5 active days before your first payout, once funded |

The risk limits work the same way as on a trading account. See [Trading Accounts](/trading-accounts) for how the fixed floor and daily limit are measured, and [Markets](/platform/markets) for the leverage limit on each pair. As you trade, your live distance to both limits is shown on your dashboard — see [Platform details](/platform).

## Who can take an evaluation

Anyone can. There is no trading résumé to submit, no minimum experience, and no approval step before you start.

## What happens when I pass

Passing is recognised automatically the moment you reach the profit target within the risk limits. There is no manual review and no waiting for someone to approve you; your trading account is activated for you to continue trading on.

## Account sizes and fees

The fee is paid once, in USDT, at the INR price shown at checkout. Account size is the reference figure your trading is measured against — not money you deposit.

| Account size | Evaluation fee | Per \$1,000 | Payout share once funded |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| \$10,000 | ₹6,600 | ₹660 | 80% |
| \$25,000 | ₹13,900 | ₹556 | 80% |
| \$50,000 | ₹27,500 | ₹550 | 80% |
| \$100,000 | ₹46,700 | ₹467 | 78% |
| \$200,000 | ₹75,500 | ₹378 | 78% |

Your payout is the share of profit you keep once you have a trading account, paid to your bank in INR. See [Payouts](/platform/payouts).

## Running more than one account

You can trade more than one account at the same time, in different sizes — useful if you want to run different strategies or sizes in parallel. The per-account fee is lower when you take more than one: the fee is reduced by 10% on two evaluations, and by 15% on three or more.

If a trading account later ends on a breach, there is a reduced-fee path to start again at the same size — see [Trading Accounts](/trading-accounts).

## What if I don't pass

If you break a risk limit during the evaluation, that evaluation ends. You owe nothing further, and there is no penalty beyond the evaluation ending. You can take another evaluation whenever you want — each one is judged on its own.

<Warning>
  **Passing is not guaranteed.** The evaluation is a real test of skill. Many people do not pass, and reaching an 8% target without breaching a 10% floor or a 5% daily limit takes discipline as well as skill. Each evaluation is judged on its own; how you did on a previous one does not decide the next.
</Warning>
