You get set up through the Wield app on Telegram, where you choose your account size, review the terms, and pay. It all happens in one place, and your access is recorded against your account.

## What happens

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Wield app on Telegram">
    The link below opens Wield on Telegram.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your account size">
    Pick a size from \$10,000 to \$200,000. The fee for each size is shown before you pay — the full list is on [Evaluations](/evaluations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and pay">
    You see exactly what you are paying for, and the terms, before you confirm. The fee is paid once, in USDT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    During early access, your spot is reserved and ready the day the platform launches. You also join the WieldAccess channel — a direct line to the team, with updates as the platform is built. You can request a full refund any time before launch — see [Early Access](/early-access).
  </Step>
</Steps>
