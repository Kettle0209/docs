## Your fee is refundable until launch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Full refund, any time before launch">
    Ask for a refund any time before the platform goes live — no reason needed — and your full fee is returned in USDT, to the wallet you paid from. The standard 7-day limit doesn't apply before launch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Held in reserve, not spent">
    Pre-launch fees sit in a segregated reserve until launch. They're not used to run the business, and stay available to refund you in full.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If we don't launch, you're refunded">
    If the platform isn't live by **15 October 2026**, you get a full refund on demand — and we'll reach out to every early member to make sure you're offered one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can always get your fee back if the platform isn't live. Reserving is reversible; that's the point.
