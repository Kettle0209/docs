## When it goes live

The platform launches by **15 July 2026**. Securing your access now makes you part of the launch cohort, with a place in the **WieldAccess channel** — a direct line to the team and the founders, with updates as the platform is built, through launch and for 30 days after.

## Your money is protected

<Steps>
  <Step title="Full refund any time before launch">
    Ask for a refund at any point before the platform goes live and you get your full fee back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your fee is held in reserve">
    Early access fees are kept in a separate reserve until the platform launches. They are not spent on running the business in the meantime.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If it does not launch, you are refunded automatically">
    If the platform is not live by **15 October 2026**, Wield contacts you and returns your full fee.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Refunds are paid back in USDT, to the wallet you paid from.

<Note>

</Note>
