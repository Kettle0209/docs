Most platforms stop at the trade. Wield runs the whole path — from your first evaluation to INR in your bank — in one environment built in house for crypto traders in India.

You start in the Telegram bot, trade in the terminal, track everything on the dashboard, and request payouts whenever you're ready. Each part is covered below.

<Note>
  Trading runs on live market data, but execution is simulated — no real assets are bought or sold, and none of your own capital is in the account. The payout you withdraw in INR is real.
</Note>
