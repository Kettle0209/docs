The Wield platform is where everything happens — your evaluation, your trading account, and the dashboard that tracks them. It is a proprietary terminal built for crypto traders.

## The terminal

The terminal is where you place your trades:

- **TradingView charts.** The terminal uses TradingView for charting.
- **Live crypto prices.** Prices are sourced from major exchanges and update in real time, so you trade against the real market as it moves.
- **Fast and responsive.** Built for active trading, with low latency between your action and the result.

## The dashboard

The dashboard is where you see where you stand at a glance:

- **Real-time risk and drawdown meters.** Your live distance to both limits — your maximum loss floor and your daily loss limit — so you always know how much room you have before a breach.
- **Integrated payout hub.** Request a payout and see your payout history in one place, once you hold a trading account.
- **Performance analytics.** A breakdown of how your trading is performing over time.

<Info>
  The platform runs in your browser and works on mobile, so you can check your account and trade from your phone or your desktop.
</Info>
