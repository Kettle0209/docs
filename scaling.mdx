You can choose any account size when you start, take more than one evaluation, and hold several accounts at the same time, in different sizes. Trading a larger account does not require starting small or working up to it.

## Choosing your size

Every account size is available from the start. A larger account is a matter of taking an evaluation at that size. Several accounts can be held at once, in different sizes — for example, to run different strategies in parallel. See [Evaluations](/evaluations).

## Earning a larger account

There is also a progression path. A trader who makes steady profit over a meaningful period without hitting a limit may be offered an account at the next size up, at no cost, with the same rules.

The sizes run in this ladder:

\$10,000 → \$25,000 → \$50,000 → \$100,000 → \$200,000

This progression is offered at Wield's discretion, based on trading record. It is not automatic and not on a fixed schedule. When it is offered and accepted, the new size applies from then on.
